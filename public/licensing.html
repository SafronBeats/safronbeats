FAQ

Questions that come up

Can I release my song on Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube?

Yes. Every tier includes monetised streaming and video rights inside its caps. Blow past them and you upgrade — what you already paid gets credited. No renegotiation, no phone call.

What is the difference between a lease and an exclusive?

A lease is non-exclusive: the beat stays available, and other artists may license it too. An exclusive purchase removes the beat from sale permanently and gives you sole right to the instrumental. Licenses already granted before the exclusive remain valid.

Do I have to credit the producer?

Yes — credit “Prod. SAFRON BEATS” wherever the song is released: streaming metadata, video descriptions and artwork notes. It is a condition of every tier.

What files do I get?

Basic: untagged MP3 320 kbps. Premium: WAV 24-bit plus MP3. Trackout: both plus a ZIP or RAR of the individual stems. Unlimited and Exclusive: everything, with no usage caps.

How fast is delivery?

Immediately. The order page unlocks downloads the moment payment clears, and it contains a printable license for each track. Keep the link — downloads stay available.

Can I get a refund?

Because files are delivered instantly, sales are final. If a file is broken or the wrong version was delivered, it is replaced or refunded — send the order number and it gets handled.

Can I use the beat in a mixtape or a free release?

Yes, within the tier's distribution caps — the caps count units distributed, whatever the price of the release. Non-profit live performances are covered at every tier.

Who owns the song?

You own the new recording you make over the beat, subject to the license. The producer retains authorship and ownership of the underlying composition and instrumental. Publishing splits can be agreed separately for bigger releases.
